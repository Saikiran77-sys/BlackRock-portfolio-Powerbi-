Global Sector Analysis & Portfolio Dashboard
Welcome to the financial command center

This project isn't just a bunch of charts; it is a deep dive into how different market sectors are performing and a granular look at specific portfolio distributions (featuring a BlackRock case study). The goal was to take raw financial data and turn it into something you can actually use to make decisions—balancing the hunger for returns with the reality of risk.

What's Inside?
This dashboard is split into three main stories:

1. The High-Level Sector Snapshot
The Big Picture: We analyzed 50 major companies across 8 distinct sectors.

Winners & Losers: Right off the bat, the data identified Consumer Staples as the highest growth sector, while Healthcare is currently flagging as the highest risk.

Return Expectations: The bar chart at the bottom ranks sectors by their Sum of Expected Return YoY. Technology is leading the pack by a wide margin (classic Tech), followed by Communication Services.

2. The Risk vs. Reward Map
Visualizing the Trade-off: This isn't your standard scatter plot. We’ve mapped sectors based on their Average Weighted Risk Score vs. their Expected Return.

How to read it: The size of the bubbles represents the Market Value.

Low Risk/High Value: You can see Consumer Staples and Communication Services floating in clearer waters.

High Risk: Energy and Healthcare are pushing further right on the risk scale (Risk scores above 4.5).

The Vibe: It uses a stylized world map background to give it that "global strategy" feel.

3. Portfolio Deep Dive (BlackRock Case Study)
The Money Shot: A detailed look at a specific portfolio with a total value of $34.32M.

Key Metrics:

Risk Score: 6.87 (Weighted)

ESG Score: 63.56 (Because sustainable investing matters)

Yield: 1.19%

Where the Cash Is: The Treemap on the right is the star here. You can instantly see that PepsiCo, Cisco, and Coca-Cola are the heavy hitters, with tech giants like Microsoft and Amazon also taking up significant real estate.

Trend Watch: The funnel chart on the left tracks the portfolio value across quarters—peaking in Q3 at $9.47M.

The Tech
Built With: Power BI (Microsoft's best friend for data viz).

Data Sources: Financial market datasets focusing on Market Cap, ESG scores, Risk coefficients, and Projected Returns.

How to Use This
Open the .pbix file in Power BI Desktop.

Filter away: Click on any sector in the bar chart (Page 1) to see how it filters the risk metrics.

Hover: Hover over the bubbles in the map view to see the exact risk/return ratios for each sector.
